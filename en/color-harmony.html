Complementary (Complementary) +180°
The color directly opposite on the color wheel, giving strong contrast and vibrancy.
Analogous (Analogous) ±30°
Colors adjacent on the color wheel, creating a smooth and harmonious feel.
Triadic +120° / +240°
Three colors evenly spaced on the color wheel, offering a balanced and vibrant palette.
Split-Complementary +150° / +210°
Uses colors on either side of the complement for a softer yet contrasting palette.
Square (Tetradic) +90° / +180° / +270°
Four colors evenly spaced on the color wheel, creating a rich and varied palette.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. What color harmony types are supported?

Complementary, analogous, triadic, split-complementary, and square (tetradic) harmonies are all supported.

Q. What is the difference between a harmony generator and a palette generator?

The harmony generator focuses on color theory relationships (e.g. angles on the color wheel), while the palette generator produces multi-swatch palettes. Both are complementary tools.

Q. Can I copy the generated colors?

Yes. Click any color swatch to copy its HEX code to the clipboard.
