Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 15, 2026

1. Data Collection

WooaColor (colorkit.wooahouse.com) has no signup or login features and does not directly collect personal data. This service operates as a static website with no server-side data storage. All color processing happens only within your browser.

2. Third-Party Services

WooaColor uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies.

  • Google AdSense: Used to display ads. Google may collect ad-related information via cookies.
  • Google Analytics (if applicable): May be used to collect anonymized visit statistics.

3. Cookies

WooaColor does not use its own cookies. However, third-party services such as Google AdSense may use cookies for advertising purposes. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

4. Local Storage

Some WooaColor features (such as controlling the PWA install banner) use the browser's sessionStorage. This data is stored only on your device and is never sent to a server.

5. External Links

WooaColor links may lead to WooaHouse network sites or third-party sites. Please review each site's privacy policy independently. WooaColor is not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites.
